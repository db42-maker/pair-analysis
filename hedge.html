How to read this
Risk removed — how much of the long's day-to-day volatility the hedge cancels out over the period (variance reduction). Higher is better.
Holds up? — the same measure on data the hedge wasn't fitted to: the hedge is sized on the first two-thirds of the period and tested on the last third. A big drop from "Risk removed" means the fit may not last.
Hedge ratio — dollars to short per $1 of the long (the hedge's beta). For the basket it's the total short, split by the weights shown.
Hedged vol — annualized volatility of long-minus-hedge, compared with the long on its own.
Worst 60d corr — the weakest 60-trading-day stretch of correlation; a low number means the relationship has broken down at times.
Coint. p — cointegration test between the long and the hedge; below 0.05 suggests the prices tend to drift back together (useful for a pair trade, not needed for a pure risk hedge).
All fits use daily returns and are backward-looking. The basket is fitted on the same data, so lean on the "Holds up?" figure.
